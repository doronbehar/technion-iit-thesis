The existence of multiple vibrational transitions in a molecule (see figure~\ref{fig:EduardusCHDBrI}), and trying to measure a specific vibration of a high energy, can cause a phenomenon termed Intra Vibrational Redistribution (IVR). The term describes the potential of the energy of an excited high-energy vibration, to be redistributed into a combination of multiple lower energy vibrations. To assess the possibility of this to happen, I wrote a small Python script that computes this. The computation method and the results are given in chapter~\ref{chap:IVR}, and were reported briefly in our published research too\cite{IVR_article}.
