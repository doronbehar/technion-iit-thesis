\chapter{Conclusion \& Outlook}

The software developed for this thesis has enabled us to explore phenomena that were hard to anticipate, and hard to analytically model. These include:

\begin{itemize}
	\item Choosing wisely laser cooling intrusion angles is significant.
	\item Rogue laser-cooled ions lead to a kinetic-energy distributions that don't follow Boltzmann statistics.

	\item The conserving of angular momentum of sympathetically cooled ions, limits their cooling rate.

	\item The sympathetically cooled ions' conserved angular momentum, depends on their initial temperature, so we identified this as hysteresis.
	\item Increasing the trap's secular frequencies, helps slightly overcome the angular momentum conservation limit, by inducing more frequent collisions with the cooled crystallized laser cooled ions.
\end{itemize}

Additionally, Baba \& Waki's analytical model~\cite{BabaWakiSympatheticCooling}, provide useful insight into the temperature dynamics of sympathetic cooling. However, since their model focuses primarily on temperature evolution, they do not account for conserved quantities such as angular momentum, and therefore cannot describe the phenomenon of hysteresis we observed.

\section*{Outlook}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The software will support future efforts to optimize sympathetic cooling in our group.
  \item The software can simulate cooling in anisotropic traps, which may further enhance efficiency. Additionally, incorporating angular momentum conservation in cylindrically symmetric traps might be feasible by modeling a $T = 0\ \mathrm{K}$ ion crystal surrounded by a dilute cloud of different-mass ions. A reliable model for the ion crystal size is available in the literature~\cite{Madelung1991} and can be generalized to non-spherical traps.
  \item The software can also be applied to optimize single-species laser cooling. Given that the cooling force~\ref{eq:laser-cool-force} depends on velocity, it would be interesting to explore time-dependent modulations of detuning or intensity to further enhance cooling performance.
\end{itemize}
